Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains the general approach to subscription fees, cancellations, and refunds for Bariq AI.

1. Subscription Nature

Bariq AI primarily provides digital access to software tools, workflows, templates, and related services. Because access may be granted immediately after purchase, refunds may be limited.

2. General Refund Position

Unless otherwise required by law or explicitly stated in writing, subscription fees and digital access payments are generally non-refundable once access has been granted.

3. Duplicate or Accidental Charges

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same transaction in error, you may contact Bariq AI for review. Verified duplicate billing issues may be corrected where appropriate.

4. Failed Access or Technical Billing Issues

If payment was completed but platform access was not reasonably provided due to a technical issue directly attributable to Bariq AI, you may contact support for resolution or billing review.

5. Cancellation

You may cancel future renewal of your subscription according to the applicable plan settings. Cancellation generally prevents future billing but does not automatically guarantee refund of fees already charged.

6. Custom Services or Manual Work

If Bariq AI later offers customized onboarding, manual setup, consulting, or special implementation services, separate refund terms may apply to those services.

7. Review Requests

Any exceptional refund request may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis, but approval remains at Bariq AI’s discretion unless otherwise required by law.

8. Contact

For refund or billing-related questions, contact: support@bariqai.com
