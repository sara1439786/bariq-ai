How the Payment Flow Should Work

Keep this early-stage payment model simple so Bariq remains manageable while you scale.

Step 1 — Business Pays Bariq Use Razorpay to collect platform payments directly into your business bank account.
Step 2 — Bariq Tracks Delivery Campaign, creator, and completion status should decide when creator payout is released.
Step 3 — Creator Paid Separately In the beginning, keep creator payouts manual or admin-approved instead of full automation.
